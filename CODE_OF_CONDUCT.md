# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including on the technical
  substance of a change, which is the point of this project.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information without consent, and other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the maintainers via
GitHub's **Report content** flow or a direct message. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and
security of the reporter of any incident.
