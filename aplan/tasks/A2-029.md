## Why

Reported in #21. `aplan start` succeeds on a task whose `paths` match nothing tracked, and
`verify` then fails on it at once with `APLAN053` under `deny_warnings`. That is the normal
state when a task's first act is to create its files, so an agent following the documented
loop hits a failure straight away.
