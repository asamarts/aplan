## Why

A post-delivery audit of `A2-026` and `A2-079` found two edge-case defects in
`crates/aplan-interop/src/docs.rs` and one unit-test gap in `crates/aplan-output/src/lib.rs`:

- **Overlapping near-prefixes lost longest-first attribution.** In `dangling`, the near-prefix pass
  correctly scanned with longest-prefix-first `variants`, but then attributed `mistyped_prefix` by
  scanning `near` (`BTreeMap`, lexical order) with `r.id.starts_with(v)`. When a plan taught both
  `A1B-v0.8` (near `A2B-v`) and `A3B-alpha` (near `A2B-`), `"A2B-"` sorted before `"A2B-v"`,
  hijacking `A2B-v0.9` and attributing `mistyped_prefix: ["A3B-"]` instead of `["A1B-v"]`.
- **Mixed numeric + hyphenated-named families truncated numeric-leading hyphenated tokens.** When
  one prefix taught both non-hyphenated numeric ids (`R-1`) and multi-hyphen named ids
  (`R-foo-bar`), `scan_numeric_tail` matched `R-1` inside `R-1-alpha` and `R-1-alpha_suffix`,
  silently accepting an unknown hyphenated token as the known `R-1`.
- **All-done `critical-path` JSON emitted `-0.0` for `path_remaining_effort_days`.** Rust's
  `Iterator::sum::<f64>()` initializes its accumulator at `-0.0`, so `unfinished_on_path` produced
  `-0.0` whenever every row on the printed critical path was `done` (`serde_json` serialized
  `"path_remaining_effort_days": -0.0`, while `serde_json::json!(0.0)` equality hid it).
- **`aplan-output` lacked direct crate-level unit tests** pinning `unfinished_on_path`,
  `remaining_matches_path_tail`, `remaining_clause`, and `render_critical_path` across all-live,
  partial-done, all-done, and divergent chains.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `dangling` attributes `mistyped_prefix` from the exact longest-matching near-prefix family
      that produced the reference, proved on overlapping numeric and named near-prefixes
- [ ] a prefix teaching both numeric ids and multi-hyphen named ids scans numeric-leading
      hyphenated tokens whole (respecting symmetric word boundaries and doubled-dash punctuation)
      rather than silently truncating them to a known numeric id
- [ ] `unfinished_on_path` returns positive `0.0` (never `-0.0`) when every row on the critical
      path is `done`, and `aplan-output` unit tests directly pin `unfinished_on_path`,
      `remaining_matches_path_tail`, `remaining_clause`, and `render_critical_path` across
      matching, all-done, and divergent chains
