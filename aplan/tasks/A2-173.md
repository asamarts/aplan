## Why

`A2-009`'s readiness gate 4 asked that every `ayaml` ceiling be proven through the product, and
it was only partly met (`docs/design/yaml-parser.md`). Each boundary is proven against
`yaml_compat` with explicit values, which proves `ayaml`'s mechanism. Through a product entry
point, only the task loader's depth boundary is proven on both sides; `parse_config` and the
task emitter refuse anchors, alias dereferences, events, documents, input bytes and output bytes
past the defaults without proving the accepted side; and scalar bytes, collection entries and tag
expansion never reach a product read in a test.

The compatibility reader also misnames a common typo. A flow collection left unclosed, such as
`tags: [a, b` followed by another key, reports "flow collection entries must be indented" at
column 1 where the bridge said "did not find expected ',' or ']'". The line is right, but the
advice sends the author to indent a line rather than close the bracket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] each native ceiling is proven through at least one product read, with its accepted
      boundary and its first refused neighbour, or the record says why one cannot be
- [ ] tests that parse task or manifest fixtures use the reader the product uses for them, the
      compatibility reader, where some use `ayaml`'s default one (`crates/aplan/src/lib.rs` tests,
      `close_gate.rs`, `amend.rs`, `version_record.rs`)
- [ ] an unclosed flow collection reports that its closing bracket is missing, fixed in `ayaml`
      and taken through a new exact pin, or wrapped by aplan where the parser cannot know
