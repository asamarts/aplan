## Why

Reported in #32 (item 3). `ready --owner` selects one owner, and `ready --unclaimed` leaves out
claimed tasks, but nothing leaves out owned tasks. In a plan where some tasks need a person,
an agent has to read the owner column and skip those rows itself.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `aplan ready --unowned` lists only ready tasks with no owner, in the style of `--unclaimed`, and
      composes with the other selectors
- [ ] #32 is closed once A2-030, A2-031 and this task are done
