## Why

Reported in #25. Each `aplan capture` prints about seventeen lines, including four snapshot
digests, and nothing an author acts on beyond the id: eleven captures printed about 190 lines.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the default human output is as short as `add`'s two lines, for example `capture I-...
      applied: <title>` and the path it wrote
- [ ] the full record remains available through `--format json` or a verbose flag
