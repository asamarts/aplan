## Why

In an adopting repository, `aplan claim ABC-001 --owner some-user` reported that `--owner` was an
unsupported argument, but the subsequent `show` exposed a new claim held by `some-user`. The command
therefore returned a usage refusal after publishing coordination state. This contradicts the
fail-before-write boundary that claims were given when they moved out of `owner` into local
ephemeral coordination across linked worktrees (ADR-0051), and makes a nonzero exit unsafe evidence
that nothing changed. The observation is retained as seen; reproducing it belongs to this task
rather than the adopter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the exact valid-id-plus-unsupported-option shape is covered through the real CLI boundary and
      either reproduces the defect or proves which observed state had another source
- [ ] every syntax/usage error is resolved before claim storage is opened or mutated, and a
      regression proves the claim store is byte-identical (or remains absent) after refusal
- [ ] valid claim, takeover, release, and identity-derived holder behavior remain unchanged
- [ ] the claim/release/start/stop CLI dispatch paths are audited for the same parse-before-effect
      invariant rather than special-casing only `--owner`
