## Why

Reported in #34. Writing `test.runners` by hand reveals its requirements one refusal at a
time: a small valid configuration takes seven attempts. `missing field schedule` refuses the
place a reader tries next and does not say the key is `test.schedule`, and a `schedule` key
on a runner is reported as unknown and then still missing. The runner deserializer drops the
key path that `aplan facts` already knows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] one error lists every missing field with its full path, for example `test.schedule`,
      `test.runners[0].list` and `test.runners[0].run`
- [ ] a misplaced key names where it belongs
- [ ] a test reproduces the sequence from #34 and reaches a valid configuration in one step
