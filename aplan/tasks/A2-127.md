## Why

`APLAN013` currently composes `is not one` with `any of the N configured runners list`, producing
"is not one any of". With two runners configured, a citation such as `test:ghost` that neither
runner lists reproduces it. The exact sentence has appeared in three adopting repositories, and one
adopter's repeated audits finally called it out after the diagnostic otherwise guided a successful
repair. The verdict and instruction are correct. The consumer-facing message is not.

Repair the composition at its source and audit the singular/plural neighbors rather than replacing
one captured string. A suggested exact name, an ambiguous name and no suggestion must all remain
grammatical while naming only the citation and runner set the predicate actually judged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] zero, one and many configured-runner forms use grammatical wording (`one of`, not `one any
      of`) and never imply a runner was queried when none was configured
- [ ] no-suggestion, one-suggestion and ambiguous/multiple-suggestion diagnostics remain actionable
      and keep the exact unresolved citation as their subject
- [ ] tests assert the message and `agent_instruction` on representative counts rather than only
      the rule code, including a suggestion whose offending candidate is not first
- [ ] the rule catalogue and changelog carry the corrected consumer text where required, with no
      rule renumbering or semantic change
- [ ] on a `done` task, the instruction names `aplan amend ID --replace-evidence FROM TO`, as
      `APLAN054`'s does, rather than only "cite" (an adopter reported this gap separately)
