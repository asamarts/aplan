## Why

An earlier foundation audit of the synthetic evaluation harness (`docs/design/tui-ai-evaluation.md`)
proved that synthetic evaluation is useful, but also that one accepted-looking run can hide
selection, leakage and transport failures. Its corrected cross-family rerun could not finish after
the evaluation host's Claude subscription reached its token limit. Running Codex and OMP alone
would exercise two clients backed by one provider family, not independent evidence.

Do not hold TUI implementation behind a temporary subscription condition and do not waive the
condition either. Re-run the evaluation after the complete CLI, engine, MCP and TUI product
exists, at several predeclared checkpoints on the public baseline. The public repository was
published from a sanitized fresh history rather than as a claim of product completeness; this
task remains a direct member of the public qualification cut so the evaluation is visible work
rather than an omitted gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] design a product-wide scenario corpus spanning first use, inspection, planning, diagnostics,
      evidence, mutation, history, Markdown, capture, automation and recovery across CLI, typed
      engine, MCP and TUI surfaces
- [ ] predeclare at least three source-bound evaluation rounds at meaningful release checkpoints;
      retain every scheduled cell and adverse attempt, and never add a retry or extra cohort
      because an outcome was inconvenient
- [ ] use Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and OMP with their then-configured default model and
      effort; record unavailable adapters without fallback and require at least two independently
      resolved provider families in the final acceptance cohort
- [ ] isolate actors from source, design intent, expected routes, hidden state, prior sessions and
      evaluator findings; prove the operating-system boundary and retain only allowlisted
      normalized evidence without credentials or reasoning payloads
- [ ] separate task actors, blind critics and deterministic oracles; counterbalance provider,
      profile, scenario and presentation order before outcomes are known
- [ ] include unfamiliar-user, expert-operator and agentic/API profiles plus hostile, stale,
      concurrent, narrow-terminal, large-plan and failure-injection fixtures
- [ ] measure completion, invalid actions, recovery, disagreement, latency, token use and grounded
      findings without presenting model behavior as human usability, accessibility or prevalence
- [ ] disposition every grounded finding one-to-one as fixed, filed or declined with evidence; a
      material fix invalidates only the predeclared affected scope and never licenses
      run-until-pass
- [ ] evaluate and document faster isolated transport options, including bounded per-session
      continuation, against the stateless baseline; adopt one only if leakage, order and provider
      quota behavior remain equivalent
- [ ] commit replayable public-safe cohorts, manifests, dispositions and an independent audit
      report for the exact release candidate; the audit recomputes eligibility and refuses
      missing, changed or single-family evidence
- [ ] keep the human evaluation evidence from A2-088 distinct and state where human and synthetic
      findings agree, disagree or cannot be compared
