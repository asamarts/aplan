## Why

Found in an adopting repository. An audit withdrew a delivered task's work, and the agent recorded
it by editing the task by hand: `status: done` back to `todo`, its evidence deleted, a blocker
added, criteria unticked. `check` passed it without a finding, as its milestone was still open.
ADR-0020 decided against exactly that, and `aplan supersede` is the route, but nothing on the
agent's path named it: `supersede` describes itself as for "a closed task whose traced outcome no
longer holds", and the task had no `traces`. The revert also deleted the two tests the task cited,
and `amend` describes `--replace-evidence` for a renamed test and `--remove-evidence` for "proof
that was never proof"; neither names proof that was real and went with the work.

Generic reproducer: `ABC-001` is `done` with two `test:` citations, in an open cut `M-one`. Edit
`ABC-001.md` to `status: todo`, delete its `evidence` block and add `blocked_by: [ABC-002]`;
`aplan check` reports nothing for `ABC-001`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] decided: `verify`, which opens the repository, reports a task whose `status` has moved
      from `done` back to a live status since `HEAD` and names `supersede`; or the decision says
      why it cannot, and where an adopter learns the route instead.
- [ ] `supersede`'s help describes it for any closed task whose outcome no longer holds, a
      delivery withdrawn or reverted after it closed included, with or without `traces`.
- [ ] `amend --remove-evidence`'s help covers a citation whose proof a later task removed with
      the work, or another verb does and the help names it.
- [ ] a test pins whatever behaviour changes, and the CHANGELOG says so.
