## Why

Reported in #57. A task's acceptance criteria or `paths` can be narrowed or widened in the same
commit that closes the task, and `close` and `verify` treat the edited claim identically to one
that was met as originally written. Rewriting a criterion is sometimes legitimate when work shows
the original wording was wrong, but nothing distinguishes an honest, stated adjustment from a
quiet redefinition of "done" or a widened `paths` glob that makes a `commit` citation easier to
satisfy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] record the design decision (ADR or HLD update) choosing between close-time comparison
      against the commit where the task left `todo`, start/claim-time stamping of criteria and
      `paths`, or a stated-reason requirement for post-start edits, preserving determinism
- [ ] implement the chosen detection so a silent rewrite of acceptance criteria or `paths` during
      closure is reported with a clear remedy
- [ ] regression tests cover unchanged criteria/paths (silent), an unstated rewrite (reported),
      and a legitimate stated adjustment
- [ ] #57 is closed
