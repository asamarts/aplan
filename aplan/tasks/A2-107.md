## Why

One adopter, running a pinned aplan build during a corrective closure, read `aplan verify --help`
advertising `--cut` as "Only this cut". Following that instruction with
`aplan verify --cut M-one --execute` exited 2: `verify` is a gate and cannot take a selection. The
current `verify --help` still advertises the same selector behavior. Keeping `check` and `verify`
whole-plan gates is an intentional, shipped decision and it is correct; the help directs an
adopter to a refused operation.

A second adopter independently followed the advertised `--tag` selector and received the same
intentional refusal, then had to discover `tasks --tag ... --format json` from the error. The
second adopter confirms this is shared help-contract friction rather than one mistaken flag.

## One outcome and non-goals

Make `check`/`verify` help accurately describe their intentional selection refusal without
weakening the gate. Shared reporting selectors remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] gate help cannot lead an adopter to expect a selected clean verdict; it either hides
      report-only selectors or explicitly states that supplied selectors are refused with a
      reporting alternative
- [ ] whole-plan `check`/`verify` retain their current positive behavior, and selected gates still
      refuse rather than qualifying a green exit code
- [ ] reporting-command selection remains usable and documented consistently; consumer-visible CLI
      regression coverage is retained and the applicable changelog/help documentation is updated
