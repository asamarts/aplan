## Why

Reported in #23. `aplan roadmap` lists cuts by the natural order of their labels and ignores
sequencing edges, by an earlier decision (ADR-0013) that refused to derive a sequence from a
label. The report asks for the reverse: display cuts in the order the work happens, ordered
topologically by sequencing edges with the label order as a stable tie-break, and list a
release after the last cut it contains, either by default or under `--order graph`.

Untestable: a decision record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] a decision is recorded, superseding ADR-0013 in part if the display order changes
- [ ] the implementation task's criteria match it, or it is dropped with the reason
