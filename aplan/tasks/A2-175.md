## Why

Under `verify --execute`, a `test.batch` adapter that exits before producing a result makes every
citation it covered unavailable. One adopting repository saw 17 `APLAN014` errors on three tasks
from one such failure. aplan attaches the adapter's captured output once, to one finding, and
tells the others "another finding in this run carries the adapter detail". That is the right
shape, but the finding that carried the output was the fifth printed, so a reader scanning from
the top met four bare pointers before the cause. The carrier is chosen in resolution order
(`batch_not_proved` in `crates/aplan-evidence/src/resolve.rs`), not in the order the findings
are printed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the adapter output is attached to the first of the affected findings in the order every
      output format prints them, and the others point to it
- [ ] a test with one failing batch adapter and citations on several tasks asserts that the first
      printed finding carries the output, in the human and JSON formats
