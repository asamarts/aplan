## Why

The final public full-profile run on exact source `d0da5b1` passed every preceding gate but measured
13,335 of 13,892 coverable `aplan` lines, or 95.9 percent, two covered lines short of the committed
96 percent floor. A raw local diagnostic had reused instrumented objects through `--no-clean` and
overstated the numerator, while the hosted gate used its owned clean allocation. Treat the hosted
result as authoritative and add stable assertions rather than lowering the floor or rerunning until
it happens to pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] the hosted/local difference is reduced to specific source lines or an equally precise cause
- [x] a test exercises the missing product behavior under the hosted environment without weakening
      an existing assertion
- [x] `coverage.json` is not lowered and both the local and hosted complete profiles pass the same
      public source revision
- [x] the retained task record links the failed run and its superseding successful run

## Audit disposition

The isolated hosted allocation exposed the untested `Chosen::Unmeasurable` refusal in
`describe_window`; its two executable lines were also visible as uncovered in the annotated local
report. `tests::version_window_refusals_preserve_ambiguity_and_checkout_cause` now asserts both the
ambiguous-release and unreadable-checkout messages. Adjacent variant audits also cover named note
citations, the inbox promotion command, and mixed id/snapshot contention.

`coverage.json` was not changed. The isolated local gate measured `aplan` at 13,406 of 13,945 lines,
or 96.1 percent. The hosted gate on exact commit `77dd6af` measured 13,396 of 13,945 lines, or 96.0
percent. All twelve crate floors and all eleven full-profile gates passed in both environments.
