## Why

Reported in #20. Parked (`blocked`) tasks count as remaining work in `critical-path`,
`summary` and `roadmap`, so a plan holding one large parked idea reports a critical path and
weeks remaining that nobody expects to spend. Parked tasks must still carry `effort_days`
(`APLAN009`). The report prefers, in order: separate parked effort on its own line; an
`--exclude-parked` flag; or documenting the behaviour and pointing at captures for work that
may never happen.

Untestable: a decision record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] a decision is recorded on how parked effort appears in the three reports
- [ ] the implementation task's criteria match the decision, or it is dropped with the reason
