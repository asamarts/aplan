## Why

Reported in #28. `export-agents-md` derives its id-family guidance from live tasks, so completing
the final task in a family makes an established convention disappear from generated policy. An
adopting repository closed the last task of one phase and saw that phase's id family silently
removed from its generated contract, with no change to its id policy.

The live-task signal was itself a repair. The generator used to pick the numeric stem with the most
tasks, so in a plan running two equally valid cohort families an ordinary task-count change
switched the generated id example from one family to the other and failed the drift gate. The
current rule names every family that still has live work, which stopped task counts from selecting
the family and excluded stray closed legacy cohorts (for example, a two-task family whose tasks are
all closed next to a family of hundreds). The replacement must preserve those properties without
making completion itself a policy change.

Generic reproducer: a plan with tasks `ABC-001`..`ABC-003` (all done) and `XYZ-001`..`XYZ-004` (two
live). `aplan export-agents-md` names only `XYZ-nnn`, although both families are the plan's
established conventions and the `ABC-` family was named while it still had live work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the design names the status-independent structural signal that makes a task-id family an
      established convention, and distinguishes two completed cohorts from a lone closed legacy
      outlier without relying on task counts or repository-specific prefixes
- [ ] generated guidance derives established task-id families from that signal while preserving
      the existing milestone, ULID, word-id, and stray-legacy-family exclusions
- [ ] regression tests prove that completing or dropping the last live task in either of two
      established families leaves both families in the generated contract and does not produce
      APLAN041 drift; a fixture with a closed historical outlier proves it stays excluded
- [ ] the changed generated-contract behavior has a proved `Behaviour`, and user-facing release
      and upgrade notes describe the corrected stable-family semantics and retire any manual
      contract-preservation workaround
- [ ] #28 is closed
