## Why

Reported in #33 (item 2). `aplan add` takes `--paths` and `--traces`; `aplan supersede` takes
`--path` and `--trace`; `aplan inbox promote` takes `--path`. Each is easy to get wrong from
memory. Related: A2-157 makes `add`'s path-list syntax unambiguous.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] every authoring command accepts one documented spelling for paths and traces, and the other
      spelling is accepted as an alias or refused with a suggestion
- [ ] existing invocations keep working or fail with a message that names the new spelling
- [ ] #33 item 2 is ticked on the issue
