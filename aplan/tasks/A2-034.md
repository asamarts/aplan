## Why

Reported in #33 (item 3). A task created by `aplan add` carries placeholder prose
(`(Why this task exists, and what is different once it is done.)` and an unticked
`state what done means for this task, in this repository's terms`) and `check` passes it.
`close` refuses the unticked placeholder criterion, so done tasks are safe, but open tasks
are what a reader of the plan sees, and a plan full of placeholders reads as planned work.

`APLAN043` already warns when the placeholder criterion survives beside criteria the author
wrote, and it is silent by design when the placeholder is alone. Its rationale, on
`check_scaffold_leftover` in `crates/aplan-core/src/validate.rs`, is "beside other criteria, never
alone": a task whose only criterion is the prompt has not answered what `add` asked, which
`APLAN038` refuses at close. Reporting the placeholder-only open task reverses that recorded
choice, so the change has to say why.
