## Why

`dep add M-two --after ABC-080` is refused correctly because a milestone's `blocked_by` is its
membership list. Cut-to-cut sequence and task-to-task dependency are expressible; "every member of
this cut waits on that task" is not. One adopting repository copied the edge to three members, and
a fourth member added later would silently miss the constraint.

This may be a deliberate limit rather than a missing primitive. The task exists to decide that
with the real recurrence in view, and to avoid overloading `blocked_by` with a second meaning if a
cut-wide prerequisite is warranted.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] an ADR decides whether a cut-wide prerequisite on a non-milestone task belongs in the model
- [ ] if supported, the relationship is stored once, applies to present and future members, is
      cycle-checked, and is distinguishable from membership and cut-to-cut sequence
- [ ] if declined, the refusal and adoption guidance name the N-edge workaround and how a new
      member is audited, rather than implying the relationship can already be expressed once
- [ ] readiness, `why`, tree, critical path, import/export and mutation semantics are covered by
      one executable matrix if the relationship is added
- [ ] no representation lets a milestone `blocked_by` entry ambiguously mean either member or
      prerequisite
